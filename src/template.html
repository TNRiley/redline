Redline

What public comment actually changed — every federal rule from 2016 onward, lined up against the version its agency first proposed.

American rulemaking has a promise built into it: an agency publishes what it intends to do, the public comments, and the agency may change its mind. Whether that second step moves the text is an empirical question nobody answers, because answering it means diffing two documents for every rule. This does that — — rulemakings paired proposal-to-final, and the regulatory text of — of them compared word by word. The median rule enters the Code of Federal Regulations with — of its regulatory text new since the proposal. Most of that is small. A quarter of it is not.
